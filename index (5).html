What it's best at

  • Clear science answers. Short, accurate explanations across physics, chemistry, biology, the human body, space, Earth, maths, and technology.
  • Following the conversation. Ask for more detail, a simpler version, or a follow-up like "how hot is it" and it stays on the subject.
  • Real-world questions. It copes with typos, casual wording, and two questions in one message.
  • Quick maths. Arithmetic, percentages, square roots, and temperature conversions.
  • Knowing its limits. When a question is outside what it has learned, it says so and doesn't guess.

How it works

Isotope is a neural network that runs entirely in your browser. Answers are instant, and your questions and chats stay on your device.

ISO-1 is the first release. It is a focused science model, not a general-purpose chatbot.
